## Model and case gates

One linear total respiratory-system compartment uses shared airway R in inspiration and expiration. No separate expiratory resistance, flow limitation, airway collapse or expiratory hold is implemented. Passive analytical auto-PEEP differs from live residual volume latched at breath start. Prescribed Pmus has no drug, drive, fatigue, injury, WOB-response or gas-exchange model; FiO2 does not simulate oxygenation, CO2 or hemodynamics.

Before an internal educator session, identify the actual served build/browser/OS/viewport/zoom; rehearse each exact selected case end to end with settings, reset/effort state, observation times, interventions, measurement provenance, screenshots, alarms and recovery. Obtain separate approval of the objective/causal explanation/interpretation and an explicit bounded session go-ahead. No approval is established by this index. Use formative usability and teaching-feasibility claims, not clinical/device equivalence or learning-effectiveness claims.

Rehearse laptop/desktop controls, both views, help dismissal, clipping and actual audio/silence if used. PR 47 addresses bounded Effort-slider geometry only. Duplicate Pmus readouts and zero-effort PC-CMVa-to-PC-CMV input synchronization remain open; other zero-effort destinations are unconfirmed. Its five groups exclude that zero-effort transition and use effort 8 before mode exits. Stop for wrong build, unreproducible state, contradictory provenance/output, a case-critical control failure or an unsupported interpretation that cannot be separated from the model.

CLIN-011, 013, 014 and 015 remain conditional gates for stronger alarm, effort, calculation or morphology claims. Broader input design, independent I-time/flow controls, automation, scoring, mobile support and audio retuning are separate product work unless rehearsal identifies a case-critical failure.
